motion & touch — the snap plan

how seventeen will move: instant, physical, one-thumb. phased so every step ships verified.

already live (build 59+)

  • horizontal paged tabs — a small drag slides between people / feed / camera / chats / you, tab words in step
  • swipe-back on every pushed screen + explicit ← everywhere
  • swipe-down discards a captured photo; card drag on the deck with rotation
  • haptic taxonomy: soft (small acts) · rigid (refusals) · heavy (peaks) · crown double-beat

phase M1 — the snap feel (next build)

  • interruptible spring physics on tab paging (response 0.32, damping 0.86 — snappy, never floaty)
  • edge-swipe OUT of any pushed screen from anywhere on the left 40% of the screen, ~24pt travel threshold — barely move a finger
  • camera as the center page: one page left/right from anywhere, cold-start < 200ms preview
  • swipe RIGHT on a chat row → opens the thread; swipe right inside thread → back (snap's signature)

phase M2 — depth & continuity

  • pull-down closes any full-screen ceremony (reveal, crown, acceptance) with rubber-banding
  • matched-geometry transitions: person name → their page; deck card → reveal
  • list rows settle with 20ms stagger on first appearance — never pop-in

phase M3 — polish

  • ProMotion 120Hz audit (no main-thread stalls; TimelineView for anything animated)
  • Reduce Motion parity for every gesture (instant states, haptics kept)
  • motion gate: XCUITest drives each gesture, screenshots midpoints, asserts frame pacing

the rule

every gesture must be interruptible, reversible mid-flight, and under 350ms to commit. nothing blocks the thumb, ever.
