the design questions

answer in any order, as specifically as you can — screenshots and pinterest links welcome. i follow your answers exactly.

1 · the feeling

  1. when someone closes seventeen at night, what's the one feeling they should be left with?
  2. what should opening the app at 8:01am feel like — christmas morning, checking a note in your locker, something else?
  3. is seventeen loud-at-school (people show each other their screens) or private-under-the-desk?

2 · the look (pinterest round)

  1. drop 5–10 pinterest images (boards or pins) that FEEL like seventeen — any medium: interiors, posters, fashion, apps.
  2. for each: what exactly do you like — the color, the texture, the type, the mood?
  3. and one image you HATE, with why — the anti-reference matters as much.
  4. current design is greige paper / mono type / one electric indigo / hand doodles. keep, evolve, or replace?
  5. photos of people: full-color, muted, or grayscale?

3 · interaction

  1. the FIRST thing a user should see when the app opens: camera (snap), the deck (tinder), or the feed?
  2. should sending an emoji feel instant (one tap) or ceremonial (hold-and-slide with the ring)?
  3. chats: keep the name+rail snap structure, or bubbles?
  4. how loud should the reveal moments be — full-screen takeovers (now) or inline?

4 · the users

  1. who is the FIRST person at beverly hills high you picture opening this — describe them in a sentence.
  2. what do they screenshot and send to the group chat?
  3. what would make them delete it in week one?

5 · what you're truly building

  1. finish this: "seventeen exists so that ______."
  2. in a year, what's the story a student tells about something that happened BECAUSE of seventeen?
  3. what's the one feature you'd keep if everything else had to go?
